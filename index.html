About

Hi, I’m Peijia — you can also call me Diga. I’m a Computer Science undergraduate at the Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering, University of Washington.

I’m interested in computer vision, multimodal learning, and human-centered AI. I want to understand how intelligent systems represent the physical world, combine different sources of information, and make decisions we can interpret and evaluate.

My projects explore explainable facial expression recognition and sensor-driven interaction. Alongside this work, I’m building DIGO, a demand-driven fashion platform shaped by my family’s experience in textile trade.

Research direction

Building useful representations of the world — and understanding how models reason with them.

01VISION / XAI

Facial Expression Recognition
& Explainable AI

Independent replication & extension · 2025–present

Reproducing a facial expression recognition study and investigating whether model explanations align with human-interpretable facial regions.

The pipeline covers 12 CNN architectures with 5-fold cross-validation, SLIC superpixels, and LIME explanations. A follow-up experiment examines alignment with FACS-based facial masks using an IoU evaluation framework.

Biocompatible Brain–Computer Interfaces
